The Mozaik Design Showroom is a renowed furniture showroom situated in Istanbul where designs from all over the world can be experienced.

02Concept

Concept & Materiality

Mozaik Design Showroom is conceived as a curated living gallery, bringing together design objects from diverse geographies in an immersive spatial experience.

Material contrast
Hard and cold materials such as raw concrete, metal surfaces and glass are balanced with warm elements such as leather, textiles and carpets.
Color usage
while the overall palette is kept neutral and calm, selected furniture pieces create anchor points in the space.
Form language
sharp architectural lines create contrast with organic and soft furniture forms.
Transparency & layering
glass partitions and double-height volume provide depth and visual continuity in the space.

The concept is based on the idea of "harmony in contrast": different eras, materials, and design languages exist in a single volume without colliding, but rather complementing each other. The existing industrial shell serves as a neutral yet characterful stage that embodies this diversity.

03Spatial

Experience and Spatial Design

The visitor experience is not linear, but based on free exploration. Upon entry, the user is greeted by a large and open space; this area has been kept simple to make the first impression strong and inviting.

Axonometric · hover to magnify
04Gallery

A neutral architectural shell lets the objects take centre stage, with statement pieces placed to create rhythm, focus and visual tension.
