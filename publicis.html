A global leader in advertising and public relations, Publicis Groupe embraces its “Power of One” philosophy by bringing a diverse range of agencies together under one roof.

02Concept

Concept & Brand Analysis

Inspired by traditional Chinese courtyards and the city’s evolving spirit, the headquarters reflect a strategic shift toward collaboration, agility and creative synergy, shaped through a bold and highly distinctive spatial identity.

03Gallery

Each floor is anchored by a central core, where stainless steel, wood and integrated amenities create a calm, continuous architectural element. Extending from this core, pantries become informal gathering points, combining red brick, lacquered wood and hand-laid tiles to bring warmth, texture and character to everyday interactions.

Publicis Groupe’s Shanghai headquarters operate as a vertical city, a creative hub shaped by the rhythm of the metropolis and the culture within. Connecting context, brand and community, the space creates an environment where ideas can emerge, evolve and intersect.
